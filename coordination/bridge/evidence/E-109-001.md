the Vault routing/specification documents. The repository existed at the
observed pre-bridge baseline; the bridge was created afterwards and remains
uncommitted/untracked. That baseline is not provenance for the bridge. The selected
named roles were found as local configuration files: `planner`, `builder`,
`researcher`, `reviewer` and `verifier`.

- required `README.md`, `tasks/`, `handoffs/`, `evidence/` and `decisions/`
  paths;
- YAML frontmatter presence in all bridge Markdown artifacts;
- unique IDs and task links;
- allowed task/evidence/decision dispositions;
- task -> handoff -> evidence -> decision references;
- cross-repository references containing repo, full SHA and path;
- absence of placeholder URLs, secret/token/credential markers, profile copy,
  raw-audio claims, live database sync and application-code paths;

`reviewer` and `verifier` agents. It is not evidence of live dispatch,
